# 社区行为准则 / Code of conduct

本项目讨论、Issue和PR应以尊重、事实和可复现问题为基础。欢迎不同背景和经验的贡献者。

Discussions, issues and pull requests should be respectful, evidence-based and focused on reproducible problems. Contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

- 讨论工作与观点，不攻击个人；避免骚扰、歧视、威胁及羞辱。Discuss ideas and work without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats or humiliation.
- 不公开他人的个人信息、企业资料、凭据或私有对话。Do not disclose personal information, workplace material, credentials or private conversations.
- 不伪造测试结果、平台验证、贡献归属或开源许可。Do not fabricate tests, platform validation, attribution or licensing claims.
- 接受有依据的纠正，允许新人提问。Accept evidence-based corrections and make room for beginner questions.

维护者可编辑或移除违反准则的内容，并按情节限制参与。争议先由维护者核对事实，再决定处理方式。涉及漏洞或安全问题的报告遵循[安全说明](SECURITY.md)。其他社区问题先向维护者请求适合的私密沟通渠道，不在公开Issue披露他人隐私。

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and restrict participation proportionately after reviewing the facts. Vulnerability reports follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md). For other community concerns, ask the maintainer for an appropriate private channel without publicly disclosing another person's private information.
