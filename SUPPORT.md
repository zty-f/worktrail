# 使用支持 / Support

先查[安装说明](references/installation.md)、[平台指引](references/platforms.md)和[共同排障](references/setup-troubleshooting.md)。English readers: see the [English quick start](references/installation.en.md) and [platform setup and troubleshooting](references/platform-setup.en.md).

通过[Issue模板](https://github.com/zty-f/worktrail/issues/new/choose)提交问题。提供版本、操作系统、Python/CLI版本、所选来源和产出、失败步骤及脱敏错误类别。使用虚构资料复现，不上传真实工作区、登录回包或公司文档。

Use the [issue templates](https://github.com/zty-f/worktrail/issues/new/choose). Include the release, OS, Python/CLI versions, selected source and outputs, failing step and sanitized error category. Reproduce with fictional material, not real workspaces or authentication responses.

登录、企业开通、文档ACL及平台CLI故障可能需要用户、企业管理员或平台上游处理。项目维护者不能替用户批准组织权限。功能建议请说明具体职场场景、已有资料格式和希望得到的结果。

Login, organization access, document ACLs and upstream CLI failures may require the user, company administrator or provider. Maintainers cannot approve company permissions. Feature requests should describe a workplace scenario, input formats and desired output.

维护者为[zty-f](https://github.com/zty-f)，不承诺在线客服或固定响应时限。安全问题请看[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)。

The maintainer is [zty-f](https://github.com/zty-f). No live support or fixed response-time promise is made. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security issues.
